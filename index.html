FAQ

Before you ask.

How soon will I see results?

Paid ads usually show early signal in 2–4 weeks, while SEO and content take 3–6 months. We agree milestones with you on the first call, and we never guarantee specific results.

Do you work with small budgets?

Yes. For paid ads we suggest at least PKR 2,000 a day (about PKR 60,000 a month) in ad spend to get useful data. If your budget is lower, we’ll tell you honestly what to do first.

Who owns the accounts and assets?

You do. Ad accounts, analytics and creative files all stay in your name from day one.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta and Google from your own account. We never handle your ad budget.

Who will I work with?

Is there a contract?

There’s no lock-in. You can cancel with 14 days’ notice, and we agree scope and price in writing before work starts.

What do the weekly reports include?

Leads, cost per lead, what we tested, what worked and what we’re changing next.
